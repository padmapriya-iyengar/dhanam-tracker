Official product guide

Track. Plan. Grow.

Dhanam Tracker helps a household record income, expenses, transfers, savings and card commitments in one shared workspace.

Start the guideData & account deletion

Important scope

Dhanam Tracker is a personal record-keeping application. It does not provide banking, payment processing, lending, investment or money-transfer services.

User-entered records

Information is entered, pasted or explicitly shared by users.

No automatic account access

Dhanam does not sign in to financial institutions or move money.

Shared household

Invited people can contribute to one household while signing in separately.

Amounts are planning aids

Always compare records with official statements before making decisions.

01

Getting started

Create your own login, then configure the household records you want to track.

Create or access an account

  1. Open Dhanam on the web or Android.
  2. Choose Create an account and register with an email and password, or choose Continue with Google.
  3. If you received a household invitation, use the exact invited email address.
  4. Complete the initial member, category and preference setup.
Google sign-in and email/password are two authentication methods. Link the Google identity to the same user where supported; do not create duplicate households unintentionally.

Dashboard

The dashboard is a monthly operating view. Changing the month changes the reporting period without changing the underlying transaction dates.

A card payment is a transfer. The purchase is counted as the expense when made; recording the later payment as another expense would double-count it.
03

Income and salary funding

Income records describe money received. Salary can fund a later operating month even when the actual receipt date is in the previous month.

Record income

  1. Open Income and choose Add Income.
  2. Select the household member and income source.
  3. Enter the actual receipt date and amount.
  4. For salary, confirm the month that income should fund. A salary received late in July can fund August without changing its July transaction date.
  5. Save and review Salary left to use on the target month.

Funding behavior

The funding month is a planning allocation, not a change to the actual income date. Other income is reported in its recorded period unless specifically allocated by supported salary rules.

04

Expenses and recoveries

Record spending against a member, category and payment source.

  1. Choose Add Expense.
  2. Select member, transaction date, category and subcategory.
  3. Enter the amount, payment source, description and optional notes.
  4. If the spending should affect another operating month, choose the available planning-month option.
  5. Save the record. Edit or delete it from Activity when required.

Recoveries

A recovery reduces the net cost of an expense—for example, a reimbursement or returned amount. Add it to the original expense so budgets and reports show the net effect.

05

Recorded transfers

A transfer records movement between two accounts. Dhanam does not execute or initiate the movement.

  1. Choose Record Transfer.
  2. Enter the actual transfer date and amount.
  3. Select the source and destination account types and records.
  4. Add a clear description such as “Credit card payment” or “Transfer to savings”.
  5. When applicable, assign the record as an expense or commitment for the next operating month.
Use transfers for account-to-savings movement and credit-card payments. Use an expense when money leaves the household and should count as spending.
06

Credit cards

Card configuration drives statement-cycle, due-month and upcoming-bill calculations.

Configure a card

  • Give the card a recognizable name and optional final digits.
  • Set cycle start and end days, statement day, and payment due day.
  • Enter the monthly budget if you want consumption tracking.

Understand the totals

  • Cards due this month uses statement-cycle assignment and payment timing.
  • Upcoming card bill sums transactions in the upcoming statement cycle.
  • Card budget left compares recorded spending with the configured budget.
  • Recorded card balance reflects purchases less recorded payments.
Statement calculations depend on correct cycle dates. Compare them with the official card statement and correct configuration differences.
07

Accounts and savings

Accounts are manually maintained records representing where household funds are held.

Account

Record the balance associated with a household member.

Savings account

Record savings, deposits or investment-like assets for household planning.

Opening balance

Establish the starting point used by balance calculations.

Archive

Hide an inactive account while retaining historical references where required.

Provider names are labels supplied by the user. Dhanam does not access the provider or validate the displayed balance.

08

Recurring commitments

Recurring records help reserve expected monthly expenses and transfers before they occur.

  1. Create a recurring item with amount and day of month.
  2. Select the responsible member, category and payment source.
  3. Review pending commitments on the dashboard.
  4. Record the actual transaction when it happens.
  5. Pause or archive a rule that no longer applies.

Pending recurring transfers are deducted from Salary left to use because the household has already committed those funds.

09

Import transaction text

Dhanam prepares a draft from text you manually paste or explicitly share.

  1. Open Import transaction text.
  2. Paste the complete transaction notification or payment message.
  3. Choose Extract transaction.
  4. Review type, amount, date, member, account and category.
  5. Correct uncertain fields before creating the record.
Privacy boundary: No SMS messages are automatically read. No financial accounts are accessed. Nothing is saved as a financial record until you review and confirm it.

Potential duplicates are flagged for review. Screenshot OCR may be unavailable in some builds and, when available, must use an explicit image picker.

10

Budgets, reports and insights

Categories

Organize expenses and create useful subcategories.

Budgets and goals

Compare recorded spending with monthly limits.

Reports

Review monthly totals, trends and category comparisons.

Insights

Use calculated patterns as prompts for review, not financial advice.

Deleted or corrected records change downstream reports and balances. Verify totals after editing historical activity.

11

Household collaboration

Each collaborator has an individual login but can contribute to the same household workspace.

  1. The household owner invites the collaborator’s exact email address.
  2. The collaborator signs up or signs in using that email.
  3. After accepting access, both users select the same household.
  4. Roles determine who can manage collaborators and ownership.
RoleTypical access
OwnerHousehold records, invitations, role management and ownership transfer.
AdminRecords and collaborator administration, subject to configured restrictions.
ContributorCreate and maintain household financial records.

Removing a collaborator removes access; it does not necessarily erase shared household records. Ownership should be transferred before an owner account is deleted when the household must continue.

12

Settings, privacy and security

  • Region: locale and display currency.
  • Appearance: light, dark or system theme.
  • Privacy mode: hide amounts from casual viewing.
  • Security and app lock: protect local access with supported device authentication.
  • Notifications: configure reminders and lock-screen amount visibility.
  • Diagnostics: inspect version, environment and scrubbed sync status.
  • Clear learned mappings: remove saved import-classification learning data.

Android permission scope

Dhanam does not declare SMS read/send, call-log, phone-state or notification-listener permissions. Authentication and network access use only the permissions required by implemented features.

13

Delete data or an account

Users can delete individual records without deleting their login, or permanently delete their account.

Delete selected data

Open a record from Activity or its feature page, choose Delete, and confirm. Accounts and configuration items may be archived when history depends on them.

Delete account

On Android, open Profile, enter the required confirmation, and choose Permanently delete account.
