Delete selected records

You do not need to delete your account to remove individual records.

  1. Sign in to Dhanam Tracker.
  2. Open Activity or the relevant Income, Expenses, Transfers, Cards, Accounts or Recurring section.
  3. Open the record and choose Delete. Where historical references must remain valid, configuration records may offer Archive instead.
  4. Confirm the action. Related balances and reports are recalculated.

Users can also clear learned transaction-message mappings from Preferences without deleting their account.

Permanently delete an account

  1. Open the Dhanam Android app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Find Delete account.
  4. Enter the requested password or email confirmation.
  5. Choose Permanently delete account and confirm.
Before an owner deletes their account: transfer household ownership if another collaborator must retain access.

What is deleted

  • User profile and login identity links
  • Authentication sessions and unused authentication tokens
  • Household membership belonging to the deleted user
  • Data solely owned by that user where deletion does not remove another user’s shared household
  • For a household with no remaining required owner, household records associated with the deleted owner may also be permanently removed

Shared household data

Records in a shared household may remain available to other active household members when the deleting user is not the sole owner of that household. Removing an account does not automatically delete information another household member is entitled to retain.

Retention

Dhanam deletes application records through the account-deletion process. Limited security logs, server backups or records required to prevent abuse may remain only for the minimum operational or legal retention period, after which they are deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Need assistance?

If the in-app process cannot be completed, contact the Dhanam Tracker developer from the support address shown on the Google Play listing. Send the request from your registered email address and include “Dhanam account deletion” in the subject. Never include your password, card numbers or authentication codes.
