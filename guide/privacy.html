Privacy

Your records remain under your control

Dhanam is a personal record-keeping application, not a banking or payment service.

Open user guideDeletion instructions

Information users provide

Account identity, household membership, income, expenses, transfers, account labels, savings records, card configuration, categories, budgets and optional notes.

How information is used

To authenticate users, maintain a shared household workspace, calculate dashboards and reports, detect potential duplicate imports, and provide requested application features.

Import privacy

Dhanam processes only text manually pasted or explicitly shared by a user. No SMS messages are automatically read and no financial accounts are accessed.

Android permissions

The app does not request SMS read/send, call-log, phone-state or notification-listener access.

User control

Users can edit or delete individual records without deleting their account. Account deletion is available from the Android Profile screen. See the deletion page for shared-household behavior.

Security

Dhanam uses authenticated HTTPS communication and supports device-level application protection. Users should protect their devices and never share credentials.
