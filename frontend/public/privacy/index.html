Dhanam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Dhanam is a family-finance application operated by Kiran Joshi. This policy explains how Dhanam handles information when you use the mobile or web application.

Information we process

Dhanam processes account information such as your name, email address, authentication identifiers and household membership. It also processes financial information you enter, including income, expenses, transfers, account balances, savings, credit-card activity, categories, budgets and recurring items. Optional descriptions or messages you submit may also be processed.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, provide household collaboration, store and synchronize financial records, calculate summaries and insights, prevent abuse, support account recovery and operate the service. If you deliberately use an AI-assisted feature, the content needed for that request may be sent to the configured AI service provider to produce the requested result.

Sharing and security

Dhanam does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be processed by infrastructure, email-delivery, authentication and AI service providers only as needed to operate features. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is restricted by authenticated user and household membership.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained while your account is active and as needed to operate the service. You can delete your account in the app. Household owners must transfer ownership before deletion when other collaborators remain. You may also use the public account-deletion page. Data may be retained only where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can review and edit financial records, manage collaborators, revoke sessions, control privacy settings and request account deletion. Contact j.kiran809@gmail.com for privacy questions.
