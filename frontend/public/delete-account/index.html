Delete your Dhanam account

Dhanam users can permanently delete their account and associated data.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Dhanam.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Open the Delete account section.
  4. Confirm using your password or account email.

If you own a shared household with other active collaborators, transfer ownership first from Household & categories → Collaborators.

Request deletion without app access

Email the address below from the email registered to your Dhanam account. Use the subject “Dhanam account deletion request” and include the account email. We may ask you to verify ownership before deletion.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

Your authentication identities, active sessions, household memberships and solely owned financial data are deleted. Data belonging to a shared household remains available to its remaining owner and collaborators. Limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.
